Writing

Mostly short memoir. Two Scholastic Gold Keys for personal essay and memoir (2024), and top 15% in the New York Times 100-Word Memoir contest.

The sky bruised red, the kind of red that stings. And I just sat. My screen flickered with muted classmates. My parents ripped apart the house, stuffing jeans, cramming socks, tossing our mundane lives into suitcases. What about my Legos? Our grandfather clock, unticking? Would it all burn? I didn't move. Didn't breathe. The fire licked its lips, devouring faded golds and muted browns of the Diablo Range.

Then, the inferno retreated, granting a reprieve. The smoke dissipated, threads dissolving into pale sky. Pandemic rhythms resumed their monotone pulse.

My seven-year-old sister split the quiet: "I wanted to go to a hotel!"
